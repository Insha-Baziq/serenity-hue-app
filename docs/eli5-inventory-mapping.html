Two shops. One master shelf.

Shopify and TikTok tell us what was sold. The master inventory tells us what physical item must go down. A mapping is the little bridge between those two facts.

The important key

The Shopify variant ID is the name tag. Titles help humans; the ID is what the app uses to find the exact mapped listing.

How the bridge is made

The page where you click “Map product(s)” writes this recipe.

Shopify saysYou chooseThe app stores
Brow Pomade · BlackBrow Pomade · BlackUse 1 physical Black variant per sale
Brow Duo · BlackBrow Pomade · Black + Brow PowderUse 1 Black pomade + 1 powder per sale

What happens after an order?

Shopify order

  • Variant ID: Black
  • Quantity: 2
→

Master change

  • Black pomade: −2
  • Powder: −2 if it is a duo

Refund or cancellation

The app looks back at the exact physical variants that were deducted and adds those same quantities back.

Not mapped yet?

The order is marked “needs mapping”. Stock is not guessed or changed until the recipe is complete.

Bundle recipe

Why the old “Default variant” was wrong

TikTok’s seven SKU quantities were being fetched, but the mapping table had only one product-level row. That row showed the total 67. The fix expands the product into seven live listing rows.

Cancel

After TikTok reports a successful or completed cancellation, the same physical quantities are restored.

Return + refund

A physical return-and-refund restores the exact variants from the original sale. A refund-only request does not automatically add stock back.

This explainer reflects the current code paths: channel quantities are fetched snapshots; the physical catalogue is the stock ledger used for order deductions.
